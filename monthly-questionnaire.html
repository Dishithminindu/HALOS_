Habitual Dietary Frequency Scoring: Rate how frequently the participant consumed each high-sodium food group over the past 30 days. This data will be transformed into feature vectors for the Random Forest ML models.
1. Processed & Canned Meats / Foods
Sausages, canned fish, ham, processed meats, meatballs, canned meats.
2. Traditional Salted Dried Fish (Karawala)
Fried dried fish, dried fish curry, kattawa, balaya dried fish.
3. Maldive Fish Sambol, Sprats & Salted Seafood
Maldive fish added to sambols/mallums, salted fried sprats (halmasso), dry shrimp.
4. Pickles, Preserves & Achcharu
Salted lime pickle (lunu dehi), Malay pickle, sinhala achcharu, mango chutney.
5. Fast Food & Devilled Dishes
Devilled beef/chicken, fried chicken, burgers, pizza, Chinese rolls.
6. Restaurant Meals & Takeaway Curries
Eating meals prepared outside home, packeted rice & curry, kottu roti, fried rice.
7. Instant Noodles & Seasoned Soups
Instant ramen, packet noodles prepared with flavor seasoning sachet, bouillons.
8. Habitual Added Table Salt & Cooking Salt
Adding extra table salt directly to cooked food on plate, dipping cut fruit in salt.
9. Salty Snacks, Chips & Papadam
Fried papadam, potato crisps, cream crackers, roasted salted peanuts / kadala.
10. Salty Sauces, Condiments & MSG
Soy sauce, fish sauce, oyster sauce, monosodium glutamate (MSG / Ajinomoto), chilli paste.
